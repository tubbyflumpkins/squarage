# Warranty Details

We take pride in the craftsmanship of every Squarage piece. To give you peace of mind, we offer a 1-year limited warranty from the date of delivery.

## What the Warranty Covers

Our warranty applies to defects in materials and workmanship under normal use for one year. This includes:

- Structural integrity of the wood base and framework.
- Proper adhesion of tile and grout.
- General craftsmanship issues that affect the intended function of the piece.

### Indoor Use

Pieces designed for indoor use are covered when kept in a typical household environment.

### Outdoor Use

Certain Squarage pieces are specifically intended for outdoor settings. These items are covered when used as directed, provided they are protected from excessive or abnormal conditions (such as standing water, prolonged direct sunlight beyond normal expectations, or exposure to corrosive substances).

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not apply to:

- Normal wear and tear (including scratches, chips, fading, or stains).
- Damage caused by accidents, misuse, neglect, improper cleaning, or modifications.
- Environmental damage from extreme weather, flooding, or other events outside normal household or outdoor conditions.
- Use in commercial settings unless expressly agreed to in writing at the time of purchase.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

- Email us at **info@squarage.com** with your order number, description of the issue, and supporting photos.
- Our team will review your claim and determine whether the issue is covered under warranty.
- If approved, we will arrange a repair, replacement, or refund depending on the nature of the defect.

## Limitations

- This warranty is non-transferable and applies only to the original purchaser.
- Any repairs or modifications made by parties other than Squarage will void the warranty.
- Our liability is limited to the purchase price of the item.
