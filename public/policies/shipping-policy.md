# Shipping Policy

At Squarage, we aim to make receiving your furniture as simple and reliable as possible. We currently ship through UPS and also offer local delivery options from our Los Feliz studio.

## Local Delivery (Los Angeles Area)

- **Free Delivery:** We offer free delivery within 10 miles of Los Feliz.
- **Extended Local Delivery:** For addresses beyond 10 miles, a small delivery fee will be applied. The fee is automatically calculated at checkout based on your distance from our studio.

## Domestic Shipping (Outside Local Delivery Zone)

- For orders outside our local delivery range, we ship via UPS.
- Shipping costs are calculated at checkout and vary depending on your location and the size/weight of the item.
- You will receive a UPS tracking number once your order has shipped.

## Processing Time

- Orders are typically processed within 5–10 business days (for in-stock items).
- Made-to-order or custom pieces may take longer, and estimated lead times will be communicated at the time of purchase.

## Delivery Issues

- Please ensure your shipping address is accurate at checkout. We are not responsible for delays or additional fees due to incorrect or incomplete addresses.
- If your package arrives damaged, contact us at **info@squarage.com** within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage so we can assist you.
